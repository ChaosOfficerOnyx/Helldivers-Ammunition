## Helldivers' Ammunition 0.8.0B (public beta)

Adds four ammo type variants (AP, Fire, EM, Subsonic) to the primary weapons, chosen from the ship Armory with a per-weapon picker and a stat preview on hover. Recommended to use in Private lobby.

**Install:** needs Arsenal / HD2MM and Bingus Shared Loader. Remove older "HD2 Enhanced Ammo" builds first.

**Known limits:** legendary and energy weapons are excluded. 20 primaries with shared weapon data get damage, armor penetration and fire rate changes only (no handling changes, no Subsonic). Full support is planned.

**Use at your own risk.** This is a client-side mod for an online game.

**Bugs:** open an issue and attach `EnhancedAmmoEngine.log` and `EnhancedAmmoUi.log`.
